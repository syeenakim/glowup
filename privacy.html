Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the GLOW UP Product Fit Check smoke test collects, why, and how you can control it. GLOW UP is an early-stage, pre-launch beta test — this policy is written in plain language for that stage, and will be expanded as the product develops. This policy is provided for transparency and does not constitute legal advice.

1. Who We Are

GLOW UP is a service operated by Meliq, an independent early-stage project based in South Korea. Meliq is not currently incorporated as a company. GLOW UP is building a tool to help shoppers check whether a K-beauty product is likely to fit their skin before they buy it. This site is currently a smoke test used to validate interest in the concept — it is not yet a commercial product.

Where this policy says "we" or "us", it refers to Meliq as the operator of GLOW UP.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on what you choose to share, we may collect:

  • Optional email address — only if you ask us to send your report or notify you about the beta
  • Optional social handle (Instagram/TikTok) — only if you choose to provide one, and only retained if you do
  • Skincare quiz responses — your biggest skincare challenge, how your skin feels and looks through the day, how it reacts to new products and to sudden weather changes, how often you experience redness or breakouts, what happens to a spot after a blemish heals, how your skin responds to sun exposure, how visible fine lines are, and your general sun and lifestyle habits
  • Gender and age range that you select from a list
  • Approximate region you select from a list (e.g., "West Coast") — not precise location or GPS coordinates
  • Device, browser, campaign, and interaction data — such as which products you viewed, general technical information about your device/browser, and which advertisement (if any) brought you to this site

Every field beyond the quiz itself is optional. You can complete the entire experience, including seeing your Product Fit Report, without providing an email address.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate your personalized Product Fit Report
  • To email you that report, only if you asked us to
  • To notify you about the beta launch, only if you opted in separately
  • For product research and to improve this service
  • For fraud prevention, security, and troubleshooting

4. Advertising and Analytics

We may use tools like Google Ads, Meta/TikTok advertising pixels, or basic analytics to understand which ads bring visitors to this site and how the funnel performs. These tools may collect general device and interaction data (for example, that "someone reached the results screen").

We do not send your email address, social handle, quiz answers, gender, age range, or region to advertising or analytics tools. Only anonymized flags — for example, which question number was reached, or whether an email was provided at all — are shared with these tools. The content of your answers never is.

5. How We Share Information

We use Google Apps Script and Google Sheets to operate this smoke test, so your responses are processed and stored through Google's infrastructure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your individual responses with advertisers or other third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain smoke test responses for 90 days from submission, after which they are automatically deleted. This retention period may change as the product develops; if it does, this page will be updated with the current period.

7. Your Choices

  • You can complete the Product Fit Check and see your result without providing an email address ("Continue without email")
  • If you opted in to beta notifications, you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in those emails
  • You can request that we delete your information by contacting us at the email address below

8. Children's Privacy

This service is intended for users age 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share, including relying on Google's infrastructure security for data storage. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

This project is operated from South Korea. Information you provide may be processed in South Korea and/or through Google's global infrastructure, which may involve servers located in other countries, including the United States.

12. Changes to This Policy

As GLOW UP moves from a smoke test to a full product, this policy will be updated to reflect new features and data practices. We'll update the Effective Date above when that happens.

This is a plain-language policy for an early-stage smoke test and is not a substitute for legal advice. If GLOW UP moves toward a public launch, fundraising, or handling of a significant number of users, we recommend having this policy reviewed by a qualified attorney familiar with U.S. state privacy laws (e.g., CCPA/CPRA) and CAN-SPAM requirements.
